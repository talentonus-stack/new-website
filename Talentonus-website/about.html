About Talentonus

Talentonus is leading manpower recruitment consultancy specialize in developing personalized staffing solutions while taking into account the work culture and requirements at the client's company. With the operations of the company handled by the industry professionals, we are able to successfully cater to the staffing requirements of all sectors from onshore to offshore locations. We believe in establishing long-term relationships with our clients by delivering value-added services of high quality.

We have huge database of applications, which acts as the foundation for the selection process based on client's requirements. Apart from this, we select applicants from varoius job portals, advertisements and social networking to increase our pool. We are maintaining very strong and premium data bank of candidates. We also prefer using robust and progressive recruitment solutions so that the full range of recruitment services can be deliver to corporate agencies and organizations. We scrutinize and screen every candidate in details before introducing them to the client. We understand that requirement of each client is unique, so we provide fresh perspective and young talent of professionals to provide a high degree of solutions.

By understanding the significance of aligning talent strategy with business strategy we tend to offer workforce solution that drive business forward. We exhaust ourselves in unique approaches and best practices to convert potential talent into future team leaders and game changers. We are technically very sound and work on the latest ERP platform and competent in providing services in various sectors like IT, Banking, etc.
